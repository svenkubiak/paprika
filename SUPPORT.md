# Support

This document describes the general support policy for the Software.

It applies to both non-commercial users and commercial licensees unless a separate written Support Agreement, Order Form, or service-level agreement states otherwise.

## General Principle

The Software is provided as a generally available product with a single public release stream.

There is no separate Community Edition, Enterprise Edition, or private support-only release channel unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Bug fixes, security fixes, patch releases, minor releases, and major releases may be made generally available to all users.

## What Is Included

The non-commercial license does not include support. Non-commercial users can use the community channels described below.

An active Commercial License includes email support as described in "Email Support for Commercial Licensees".

Beyond that, neither the non-commercial license nor the Commercial License automatically includes:

* guaranteed resolution times;
* guaranteed bug fixes;
* guaranteed security fixes;
* guaranteed release schedules;
* custom development;
* consulting;
* architecture reviews;
* migration assistance;
* phone, chat, video, or on-site support; or
* service-level agreements beyond the response time stated in this document.

Any such commitment must be expressly agreed in writing.

## Community and General Support

Users may report bugs, request features, ask questions, and submit contributions through the publicly available channels provided by the project.

These may include:

* the public issue tracker;
* public discussions;
* community forums;
* source-code repository discussions;
* public documentation; or
* other community channels.

Responses are provided on a best-effort basis.

There is no guarantee that:

* a report will receive a response;
* a reported issue will be reproduced;
* a bug will be fixed;
* a feature request will be implemented;
* a workaround will be provided; or
* a response will be provided within any particular time.

## Email Support for Commercial Licensees

### Eligibility

Email support is available to Licensees with an active Commercial License for the duration of the License Term. A support request must name the Licensee and, where available, the order or invoice reference.

### Channel

Support requests are sent by email to:

hello@getpaprika.dev

Requests can be written in German or English. Commercial licensees do not need to use the public issue tracker, although they may.

### Response Time

The Licensor provides an initial response within 24 hours on Business Days.

A Business Day is Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays observed nationwide in Germany.

The response period only runs on Business Days. A request received on a Friday afternoon is therefore answered by the same time on the following Monday at the latest. A request received on a day that is not a Business Day is treated as received at the start of the next Business Day.

The initial response is a qualified reply by a person, for example an answer, a request for further information, or an assessment of the issue. An automatic acknowledgment does not count as an initial response.

A response-time commitment is not a guarantee that an issue will be resolved within the same period.

### Scope

Email support covers:

* installing, configuring, and upgrading the Software;
* operating the Software within the licensed scope; and
* investigating suspected defects in the Software.

Email support does not cover:

* the Licensee's own application code;
* third-party software, services, or infrastructure, except where it directly concerns running the Software;
* custom development, consulting, or architecture reviews;
* migration projects; or
* phone, chat, video, or on-site support.

Support is provided for the latest generally available release. Resolving an issue may require upgrading to that release.

### Absences

Planned absences of the Licensor are announced to commercial licensees at least seven days in advance. During an announced absence, the response period is extended to three Business Days.

During an unforeseen absence, such as illness, the response period is extended accordingly, and the Licensor responds as soon as reasonably possible.

### Missed Response Times

Missing a response time does not entitle the Licensee to refunds, service credits, or damages unless expressly agreed in writing.

### Changes to Email Support

The Licensor may change this section for future License Terms. A change does not reduce the support of a License Term that is already paid for. For monthly licenses, a change applies from the next billing period.

## Bug Reports

Bug reports should include sufficient information to reproduce and investigate the issue where reasonably possible.

Useful information may include:

* Software version;
* operating system;
* runtime environment;
* deployment method;
* relevant configuration;
* logs;
* reproduction steps;
* expected behavior; and
* actual behavior.

The Licensor may request additional information before investigating a report.

Submitting a bug report does not create an obligation to provide a fix.

## Bug Fixes

The Licensor determines, at its discretion:

* whether an issue is classified as a bug;
* the severity of the issue;
* whether the issue will be fixed;
* when the issue will be fixed;
* which release will contain the fix; and
* whether a workaround is sufficient.

Bug fixes may be included in any generally available release.

A fix may require upgrading to a newer Software version.

## Security Issues

Security vulnerabilities should not be reported through public channels where doing so could expose users to unnecessary risk.

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities according to `SECURITY.md`.

The Licensor may investigate and address reported vulnerabilities at its discretion unless a separate written agreement provides otherwise.

No specific remediation time is guaranteed unless expressly agreed in writing.

## Security Fixes

Where the Licensor chooses to provide a security fix, the fix may be released as:

* a patch release;
* a minor release;
* a major release;
* a configuration change;
* a mitigation;
* updated documentation; or
* another generally available update.

The Licensor does not guarantee that security fixes will be backported to older releases.

Users may be required to upgrade to a newer Software version to receive a security fix.

## Supported Versions

Unless otherwise announced by the Licensor, only the latest generally available release is considered actively maintained.

Older releases may continue to function but may not receive:

* bug fixes;
* security fixes;
* compatibility fixes;
* dependency updates; or
* technical assistance.

The Licensor may publish a separate supported-version or end-of-life policy at any time.

## Releases

The Licensor may publish releases according to its own development schedule.

There is no commitment to publish:

* patch releases at fixed intervals;
* minor releases at fixed intervals;
* major releases at fixed intervals;
* long-term-support releases; or
* emergency releases.

Release frequency may change at any time.

## Backward Compatibility

The Licensor aims to maintain reasonable compatibility where practical, but does not guarantee backward compatibility unless expressly agreed in writing.

Major releases may include breaking changes.

Minor or patch releases may also include changes necessary for:

* security;
* correctness;
* compatibility;
* legal compliance;
* dependency updates; or
* architectural improvements.

Users are responsible for reviewing release notes and testing upgrades before production deployment.

The Licensor does not control third-party products and does not guarantee continued compatibility with them.

Changes to third-party dependencies may require changes to the Software or to user deployments.
